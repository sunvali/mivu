## Description (4000)

Mivu is a private food diary with a friendly coach. Tell it what you ate in your
own words, take a photo, or speak, and Mivu records the meal with estimated
calories, protein, carbs and fat. Every estimate is yours to review and correct.

LOG THE WAY YOU TALK
• Describe a meal in plain language: "two eggs, toast and a coffee with milk".
• Add photos of your plate, or speak and review the text before sending.
• Record morning weight and workouts in the same conversation.
• Ask Coach to fix an amount or a food, and it updates your diary.

SEE YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• Today shows calories and macros against targets that fit you.
• The Diary keeps every day, with photo-led meal details.
• Progress charts your weight and intake over time.
• Calories left appear in a widget, on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
• Log Food is one tap away from the Home Screen, Control Center and the app icon.

MEET PIP
Pip, Mivu's fox companion, sets up your daily targets in about a minute and
reacts as you log. Pip respects Reduce Motion.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No Mivu account. Your diary and photos are stored on your iPhone.
• No ads, no tracking.
• Back up to a folder you choose, or export your diary and photos at any time.

AI THAT USES YOUR OWN PLAN
Connect your ChatGPT account to estimate nutrition and chat with Coach. Requests
use your ChatGPT plan, and you can manage usage or disconnect in Settings. Mivu
asks before anything is shared with OpenAI. On supported iPhones, Apple
Intelligence can log meals on-device without an account.

Nutrition figures are estimates, not medical advice. Talk to a health
professional before making medical or dietary decisions.
