# Russian (ru) glossary

Written before the translation; every Russian string follows it. Shipped as
bare `ru`, for Russian speakers anywhere, including Israel and the USA. The
owner reads every string on one page before the language ships.

## Voice

- **Register:** ты, lowercase. Never вы. Warm, short, everyday; imperatives
  where natural ("Попробуй ещё раз").
- **Gender-neutral toward the person.** Russian marks gender on the past tense
  and short adjectives, so never "ты добавил(а)", "готов(а)", "уверен(а)".
  Use passive or impersonal forms, nouns or present tense:
  "Ужин добавлен", "Цель достигнута", "Съедено: 1 240 ккал",
  "Обед пока не записан", "Продолжаем?", "Так держать".
- **Buttons and menu items:** infinitives or nouns, as Apple's Russian writes
  them: "Сохранить", "Отменить", "Добавить еду".
- **Errors:** "Не удалось …", then what to do.
- **Pip:** never in a gendered past tense of its own; present tense,
  "Давай…", "Готово", "Записано", first-person plural ("Давай проверим").
- **Tone:** plain everyday Russian; no bureaucratic forms ("осуществить",
  "данный") and no "залогировать".

## Names

- **Mivu**, ChatGPT, OpenAI, Apple, Apple Intelligence, Siri, USDA and iPhone
  stay in Latin letters, undeclined: "в Mivu", "в ChatGPT".
- **Pip** is «Пип», declined as an animate masculine noun: Пип, Пипа, Пипу,
  с Пипом, о Пипе ("Спроси Пипа"). Pip is a fox. The sentence about Pip may be
  masculine; Pip's own lines still avoid gendered past tense.
- **Coach** (the tab and the feature) stays "Coach", in Latin letters.
- **Snap It** stays in English, undeclined, after a noun where grammar needs
  one: "в Snap It", "режим Snap It".

## Siri phrases

Imperative ты ("Спроси Пипа в ${applicationName}"). Every shortcut that names
Snap It also has a phrase without English ("Запиши еду в ${applicationName}").

## Plurals

Russian needs `one`, `few`, `many` and `other`: 1 день, 2 дня, 5 дней,
1,5 дня. Every form keeps the number (`one` covers 21, 31, …).

## Numbers and dates

Decimal comma, a non-breaking space between a number and its unit, and a space
for thousands, all from the system formatters. Day before month
("15 ноября"). 24-hour time unless the phone says otherwise.
